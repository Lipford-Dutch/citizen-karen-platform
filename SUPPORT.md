# Support

Citizen Karen is an open-source civic-tech demonstration maintained by the Lipford-Dutch organization. It is not a government service, legal service, emergency channel, or complaint destination.

## Choose the right channel

- **Questions and implementation discussion:** use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Lipford-Dutch/citizen-karen-platform/discussions).
- **Reproducible bugs:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/Lipford-Dutch/citizen-karen-platform/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) using synthetic or redacted data only.
- **Product ideas:** start an [Ideas discussion](https://github.com/Lipford-Dutch/citizen-karen-platform/discussions/categories/ideas) before opening an implementation issue.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** use a [private security advisory](https://github.com/Lipford-Dutch/citizen-karen-platform/security/advisories/new).
- **Real complaints or government services:** use the official destination linked by the directory; do not post complaint content here.

Do not include names, contact details, complaint narratives, tracking identifiers, credentials, or other personal information in public issues or discussions.

Maintainers cannot provide legal advice, emergency assistance, or status updates for complaints submitted to a public agency.
