# Code of conduct

Citizen Karen contributors are expected to make participation respectful, safe, and accessible.

## Our standards

Use welcoming language, respect differing experiences, give actionable feedback, protect personal information, and focus disagreements on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private data, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
